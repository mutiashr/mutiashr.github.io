K-Means Clustering for F1 Driver Performance Analysis (2024 Season)
Jurnal Buffer Informatika · October 2025 · Lead Researcher · Unsupervised Learning
+
RESEARCH QUESTION
Championship standings conflate single-race outliers with season-long consistency. Can unsupervised clustering group the 2024 Formula 1 grid into objective performance tiers across multi-dimensional season race metrics?
METHODOLOGY & RATIONALE
Standardized 2024 season race metrics in Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn). Applied the Elbow Method (inertia inflection) to select k = 4 tiers and evaluated cluster cohesion vs. separation using Silhouette Score (0.5735).
FINDINGS & LIMITATIONS
Identified 4 distinct driver tiers across the 2024 grid. Scope limitation: A 0.5735 Silhouette Score reflects moderate geometric separation in season-aggregate feature space; it does not isolate driver skill from constructor car pace or lap-level telemetry.

ML Algorithm Comparison for Tokopedia Cosmetic Sales Prediction
JURTEKSI · September 2025 · Thesis Research · Supervised Classification & SMOTE
+
CLASS IMBALANCE PROBLEM
E-commerce marketplace sales on Tokopedia exhibit structural class skew: high-volume bestsellers represent a small fraction of listings, causing standard classifiers to over-predict the majority class.
EXPERIMENTAL PIPELINE
Applied SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) prior to training 4 classifiers (Random Forest, XGBoost, Logistic Regression, and Naive Bayes), tuned via GridSearchCV and evaluated on Accuracy and F1-Score.
WHY RANDOM FOREST WON
Random Forest achieved the strongest generalization (85.3% Accuracy, 0.75 F1-Score) by capturing non-linear interactions across price, rating, and review attributes on SMOTE-balanced partitions without overfitting.

LPG 3KG Distribution Clustering: Palembang Subsidy Equity Analysis
Jurnal Buffer Informatika · April 2025 · Unsupervised Spatial Equity Analysis
+
PUBLIC POLICY QUESTION
Subsidized 3kg LPG is distributed through official sub-district outlet networks (pangkalan) in Palembang. Do official outlet counts scale proportionally with sub-district population density?
CLUSTERING PIPELINE
Cleaned administrative sub-district population and official LPG outlet records in Python (Pandas), determined optimal cluster count via the Elbow Method, and segmented sub-districts with K-Means (Scikit-learn, Matplotlib).
FLAGGED AREAS VS. LIMITATIONS
Flagged high-population sub-districts such as Sukarami for exhibiting lower outlet-to-population ratios relative to peer clusters. Scope limitation: Flags spatial administrative ratios for policy review rather than proving household shortages, as informal retail flows and household demand micro-data were outside dataset scope.
